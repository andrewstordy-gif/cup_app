## Acceptance criteria

- Define Release 1 entities, identifiers, relationships, ownership, lifecycle states, and invariants for Organisation, Host/User, Account-Free Participant, Session, Sample, Cup, Cup Assignment, Response, Tasting Event, Share Attempt/Outbox Item, and Audit Event.
- Make session form/version and blind/open mode session-level contracts, consistent with the Product Specification.
- Separate a coffee Sample from one-or-more physical Cup Assignments and prevent one cup from having two active sample assignments in the same session.
- Define stable, collision-resistant local identifiers for sessions, participants, responses, events, assignments, and share operations, including idempotency-key rules.
- Define local transaction and acknowledgement boundaries so an acknowledged observation survives restart, sleep, and offline operation.
- Define participant result states: private/local, complete, queued, sharing, shared, and failed, with explicit consent required for every share request and no automatic end-of-session upload.
- Define retry, deduplication, acknowledgement, conflict, and replay behavior for idempotent synchronisation without selecting a backend vendor.
- Define blind-safe host, participant, NFC, cached, log/analytics, and network projections. Protected identity must not be present in participant-readable data before reveal.
- Define the minimum offline session package needed for NFC, QR, and manual-code joining and continued tasting without connectivity.
- Define session-scoped participant access and expiry as a configurable policy, leaving the Product Owner's duration decision explicit.
- Define an additive, migration-safe path from the current SQLite prototype, including preservation of existing sessions and feedback, rollback/recovery expectations, and legacy-data ambiguity handling.
- Identify current prototype/specification conflicts and classify each as migration work, implementation defect, unresolved product decision, or out of scope.
- Keep FR-028B explicitly unresolved; do not invent production form enum values or compatibility policy.
- Distinguish Release 1 contracts from Release 2 Smart Cup timing, sensor, and AI scope.
- Provide a dependency map for subsequent implementation tasks and a concise set of testable invariants suitable for automated migration, offline, idempotency, and blind-isolation tests.

## Decisions

- The Product Specification is authoritative where the current database, README, tests, or source disagree.
- The contract should remain provider-neutral. Backend, authentication, billing, and hosting selection require a later reviewed decision and Product Owner approval where credentials, cost, or difficult-to-reverse architecture are involved.
- FR-028B remains a blocking Product Owner/protocol decision and is excluded from resolution in this task.
- The task may document open policy parameters but must not silently choose product policy for participant expiry, reopen grace, retention/deletion, pricing/trials, PDF delivery, export schema, or supported launch tags.
- New domain identifiers use cryptographically random 128-bit UUIDs; the protocol-constrained, securely generated 14-character `public_session_ref` remains a separate identifier.
- Local tasting changes use append-only stable events plus a transactional response projection. The UI acknowledges a change only after the durable local transaction commits.
- Each explicit **Share results** action creates one immutable consented snapshot and Share Operation. Retries reuse its idempotency key; later edits require a new explicit share action.
- Blind and tenant-safe views are allowlisted projections at persistence/serialization boundaries, not UI-only filtering.
- Prototype migration is additive, idempotent, backed up, and reversible. Ambiguous legacy records are preserved and marked for resolution rather than guessed or discarded.

## Review findings

- **Outcome:** **CHANGES REQUIRED** — independent non-author architecture and QA/invariant review of exact branch tip `ce2ffd7e1f74ed837b05acb3312af572f38c49ff` and contract implementation commit `30c686c724318c09f46dbfd5088acc13fe569298`.
- **Review scope:** Authority compliance; every R1-001 acceptance criterion; domain/entity correctness; migration feasibility; offline, durability, conflict, and idempotency invariants; Release 1/Release 2 scope discipline; and whether later tasks can implement the contract without inventing incompatible behavior. Dedicated security/privacy approval, physical NFC behavior, production migration execution, and prospective/post-merge validation were not performed by this review.
- **Findings by severity:**
  - **HIGH — Cold offline NFC join has no complete identity/access reconciliation contract.** `NDEF_PROTOCOL.md` supplies a 14-character public session reference, sample position, form enum, mode, and display metadata, but no domain `session_id`, `sample_id`, `cup_assignment_id`, form version, or independently issued participant grant. Contract sections 5.8 and 10 require those domain references and an offline package, while section 12.2 exposes `joinWithNfc` without defining how an unprepared participant device derives provisional records, validates the intended session-scoped capability, obtains the immutable form version, or reconciles those local records later without duplicates or loss. This leaves FR-002, FR-003, FR-027, FR-030, and FR-061 implementers with mutually incompatible choices.
  - **HIGH — Cup reassignment is not crash-safe across the database/NFC boundary.** Section 5.7 requires ending the prior assignment and activating the new assignment in one local transaction *before* the separate physical write/verification. A crash, cancellation, or failed write can therefore leave the local resolver pointing at the new active assignment while the tag still carries the old payload. The visible `tag_write_status` does not define which assignment resolves, whether the old assignment remains authoritative, or how retry/rollback recovers. That is insufficient for deterministic resolution and FR-028A's requirement that the new assignment resolves after successful write/verification and interrupted writes never report success.
  - **HIGH — A consented multi-response share snapshot is not deterministically defined.** Revisions are defined per Response stream (section 4.3), but a Share Operation has one `snapshot_revision` and includes complete responses/events “through” that revision (sections 5.10 and 8.1). The contract does not define a participant/session aggregate sequence or revision vector, so concurrent edits across multiple responses do not have an unambiguous atomic cut. It also requires a `canonical` hash without defining canonical serialization, field ordering/normalization, hash algorithm, or server verification rules. Different mobile and service implementations could hash different bytes, accept a torn snapshot, or disagree on duplicate-versus-conflict behavior, undermining FR-064 and invariants 15–19.
  - **MEDIUM — Deterministic lookup identifiers lack required uniqueness and normalization rules.** Section 4 requires secure generation for `public_session_ref` and calls physical NFC identifiers “normalized,” but it does not define the normalization contract or require uniqueness/collision rejection for either lookup key. Cup Assignment uniqueness cannot prevent two Cup rows representing the same tag, and random generation alone does not make a public session reference a unique database key. This weakens deterministic cup/session resolution required by FR-020, FR-023, and FR-027.
- **Specification/test evidence:** `docs/PRODUCT_SPEC.md` section 6.3 and FR-027 require an NFC tap to add and open the session locally without a network request; FR-003 and section 10.3 require locally creatable session-scoped access; FR-030 requires the correct immutable form; FR-023 and FR-028A require deterministic resolution after verified assignment and safe interrupted writes; FR-039/FR-064 and sections 9.2/10.2 require durable, conflict-safe, idempotent local capture and explicit sharing. `docs/NDEF_PROTOCOL.md` sections 5–8 confirm the physical payload lacks the domain IDs and form version the proposed offline/Response model requires. Current prototype evidence in `src/data/localDatabase.js`, `src/data/sessionRepository.js`, and `docs/DATABASE_SCHEMA.md` confirms migration must bridge the legacy session/sample/cup conflation and insecure ID fallbacks rather than relying on an existing identity layer. Scope discipline otherwise passed: the implementation commit adds only the proposed contract; it keeps FR-028B unresolved, does not select providers or policy values, separates Sample/Cup/Assignment, and does not introduce Release 2 behavior.

Add invariants for first-device NFC join in airplane mode and reconciliation.
  2. Add concurrent-edit and cross-implementation hash vectors to invariants.
