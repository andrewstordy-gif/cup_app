## Acceptance criteria

- All three authoritative documents consistently state that NDEF4 may contain the real coffee name/origin and processing metadata in blind mode.
- The participant-facing Cup App UI, accessibility output, previews, and ordinary participant workflow hide protected coffee metadata until that participant explicitly completes the session.
- Participant completion reveals the locally held coffee metadata for that participant and the reveal remains visible on that device if the participant reopens or edits results.
- Completion and reveal do not create share consent, an outbox item, or an upload; **Share results** remains a separate explicit action.
- The documents explicitly state that preventing deliberate inspection with a third-party NFC reader is not a Release 1 requirement, without weakening protection of participant results, credentials, tenant data, or other secrets.
- Host/global reveal language is reconciled with per-participant completion so there is no contradictory trigger or implied need for internet, tag rewrite, or reveal QR.
- Blind identifiers continue to drive the tasting workflow before completion, and the app does not visibly expose real metadata early.
- The NDEF protocol is clarified without inventing FR-028B enum values or changing the existing payload shape.
- Existing unrelated requirements remain unchanged.

## Decisions

- Product Owner approved per-participant reveal on explicit session completion.
- Real coffee metadata may remain in NDEF4 during blind sessions.
- The Release 1 threat model does not attempt to prevent a participant from deliberately inspecting the raw tag with another NFC-reading application.
- Reveal is permanent on the participant's device after completion.
- Completion/reveal and explicit result sharing are separate actions.
