   scores/NDEF flags. Legacy remains supported Open-only; mixed CVA per-sample
   modes preserved. Unsupported/foreign/damaged records fail closed.

   reactivated sample is blank; active re-scan preserves saved results and no
   duplicate. Scan/open/typing alone does not create saved-score progress.
   Inactive card cannot activate/score via tap, route or stale form save.
3. Confirm local removal clears only target's saved scores/notes/recovery,
   retains known card grey with same code. No cup write/setup/other profile or
   other session change. Durable selection/removal epochs fence stale Save,
   queued Legacy recovery/Leave, scan and completion callbacks including ABA.
   Whole-session deletion cleans selection sidecars without resurrection.

   sample, all active required saved scores, ignore grey; clear immediate missing
   warning. Qualify reveal against exact current actor/saved revision/hash/active
   coverage/removal epochs; only active Blind details reveal. Saving changed
   scores OR notes, activating/reactivating/removing after COMPLETE invalidates
   completion and re-hides; open/no-op/discard preserves COMPLETE. Keep history
   separate from current progress so clearing scores never grants setup or
   reveals. Preserve accepted creator behaviour (current-rating Pending reset).

   neutral Complete/Scan and completed disabled-upload/Scan footers, compact
   title, sample-code header and Not uploaded labels in Details/list. No actual

   completed CVA/Legacy edit Save/Stay/Leave semantics. No unrelated form changes.

   evidence exactly. Any enrollment/backfill of existing participant records
