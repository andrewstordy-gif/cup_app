## Acceptance criteria

- New eligible CVA Sessions pin exactly one approved bundled `f=1` definition; existing 1.0.0 Sessions and responses retain their original interpretation. Add a reviewed successor definition for the optional defect-description input; preserve the existing rating set, defect type semantics, 1–8-cup inputs, score formula, rounding and five-cup-equivalent labels. Unknown/mismatched pins fail visibly rather than falling back. No NDEF field or numeric enum changes.
- Present Fragrance, Aroma, Flavour, Aftertaste, Acidity, Sweetness, Mouthfeel and Overall with the existing 1–9 buttons, established header, restrained typography, and thin horizontal section dividers. Do not redesign the successful scoring input itself. Use English spelling consistently.
- Move Uniformity/non-uniform cups from the Defects drawer into the always-visible main form, below the ratings, without changing the existing selection data or `10×u/n` deduction meaning. For one cup, preserve any canonical one-cup rule. Show the current count/impact clearly without implying Uniformity is an additive Legacy-style score.
- The CVA Defects drawer shows Defective Cups first, the existing defect-type choices, an editable free-text **Describe the defect** input, and a live **Score deduction**. Description and defect-type choice do not change the numeric deduction; only the assessed defective-cup set and trusted cup count do. For `n` cups and `d` defective cups display the unrounded deduction `20×d/n`; the final CVA total rounds once according to the immutable scorer. Avoid negative zero. Description persists and restores offline, including after force-close, without coupling it to rating notes or inventing a score effect.
- Show **Overall score** once near the bottom of the form using the approved form scorer and persisted current inputs. Do not fabricate a complete score when mandatory ratings or cup judgments are missing; show an incomplete/pending state instead. On non-five-cup totals, show the required adapted-score label. Do not use the illustrative 94.00 from the design preview as a constant.
- Keep the established blue **SCAN CUP** fixed footer behavior. A scan does not imply completion, data sharing, or loss of unsaved work. Preserve the current explicit save/completion path and CVA autosave/regression behavior; do not turn this into a broad navigation rewrite.
- Automated evidence covers old/new form pin behavior, score unchanged for equivalent old/new responses, 1/3/5/8-cup deductions and adapted labels, defect-description persistence/reopen and isolation from rating notes, incomplete-versus-complete overall score, Uniformity relocation, accessibility labels/targets, and CVA regression.
