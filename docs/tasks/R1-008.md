## Proposed immutable form-definition design — independent review required

This is a pre-implementation design, not an approved production schema, UI, NFC change, or certification of the prototype. The implementation author and independent source/architecture reviewer must verify the source references and exact frozen manifest before code. A draft response may contain absent judgments; **completion and final-score calculation** require all mandatory judgments below. State semantics are field-specific and must be explicit: for mandatory ratings and cup sets, an absent key or `null` means **unassessed draft**, while an empty cup array `[]` means **assessed, none marked**. For the two defect classifications only, an absent key means unassessed draft, but an **explicit `null` means assessed, no scored defect**, provided the companion cup state is explicitly assessed empty. Optional observational fields may be absent or `null` without blocking completion. Never impute an assessed value or score from absence/`null` on a mandatory field. At completion, no required field may be absent or `null` except the explicitly permitted no-defect classification. Cup identifiers in a form response are stable one-based physical positions `1…n`, not NFC UIDs; cup sets serialize as ascending, unique integer arrays and reject duplicates, non-integers, and positions outside declared `n=1…8`. Preserve the original judgments and definition identity with every response; derived totals are reproducible projections, not substitute observations.

### CVA Affective response contract (`f=1`)

Source: SCA Standard 104-2024 sections 5.1–5.5 and Appendix 7.2; eight 9-point impression-of-quality sections, affective notes, cup-wise non-uniform/defective marks, and one of three named sensory defects. Its printed form also has name/date/purpose contextual headings; participant identity and time come from the response/session envelope and an optional session purpose can be recorded there, not fabricated as additional numeric assessment fields.

| Canonical field | Type and allowed values | Completion rule / source meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `ratings` | Fixed, ordered mapping `fragrance`, `aroma`, `flavor`, `aftertaste`, `acidity`, `sweetness`, `mouthfeel`, `overall`; each an integer `1…9` | All eight required to complete; exact section distinction, including separate fragrance and aroma, is mandatory. Store final selected rating, not every interim mark. |
| `affective_notes` | Optional text for each printed group: `fragrance_aroma`, `flavor_aftertaste`, `acidity`, `sweetness`, `mouthfeel`, `overall` | Never scored; may be blank. Extra free-form observations must not be mislabelled as another CVA assessment. |
| `non_uniform_cups` | Explicit set of physical cup positions | Required as an assessed set at completion; marks qualitative cup differences, not mere intensity/brewing variation. For `n=1`, must be empty. |
| `defective_cups` | Explicit set of physical cup positions | Required as an assessed set at completion; may be empty. Count is derived from set cardinality, never entered separately. |
| `defect_type` | Explicit `null`, `potato`, `moldy`, or `phenolic`; absent in an unfinished draft only | At completion, `null` iff `defective_cups=[]` has been explicitly assessed; exactly one of the three source types when nonempty. An absent `defect_type` is **not** equivalent to `null`/no defect. Other descriptive words may be notes, not scored as a CVA defect. |
| `evenly_defective_all_cups` | Explicit Boolean | Required at completion. `false` when `defective_cups=[]` or a proper subset; `true` only when all cups are defective and the defect is judged even across cups. For `n=1` with the sole cup defective, it must be `true`. |

For nonempty defective set, every defective cup must also be in `non_uniform_cups`, **except** when all `n` cups are defective and evenly defective; in that case the defect itself does not force those cups to be marked non-uniform, although separate qualitative differences may still be marked. Do not infer the exception merely from `d=n`: the taster must explicitly indicate whether the defects are even across all cups. For `n=1`, a defective cup is necessarily the even-all-cups case and the non-uniform set is empty. For `n>1`, a false even-all-cups value enforces defective-set inclusion in non-uniform set. The source allows other non-uniform cups not defective. This design keeps the source's coherence rule enforceable without guessing.

Let `H` be the sum of the eight integer ratings, `u=|non_uniform_cups|`, and `d=|defective_cups|`. Compute the exact rational pre-rounding score `S = 21×H/32 + 211/4 − 10×u/n − 20×d/n` (equivalent to SCA-104's `0.65625×H + 52.75 − 2u − 4d` at `n=5`). Round `S` **once**, to nearest quarter point; exact halfway ties round upward, matching the source's appendix table (e.g. `H=12` gives `60.75`, not `60.50`). Represent the final score in integer quarter-points and display two decimals. Do not clamp an otherwise valid low score. At `n≠5`, every total view/export must visibly say `Adapted — five-cup-equivalent Cup App score`; at `n=5`, identify the SCA-104 Affective score without an adaptation claim. The source specifies quarter-point rounding; the explicit tie direction is an implementation-design rule corroborated by its table, not new Product Owner policy.

Required score/validation fixtures: eight 5s, `n=5`, empty sets → `79.00`; eight 9s, `n=5`, empty sets → `100.00`; `H=12`, `n=5`, empty sets → `60.75` tie; eight 5s, `n=5`, one defective cup also marked non-uniform → `73.00`; eight 5s, `n=1`, sole cup defective, even-all-cups true and no non-uniform mark → `59.00` adapted. Reject defect type without cups, cups without defect type, a partially defective set not included in non-uniform marks, an unmarked all-cups exception, out-of-range ratings/cup IDs, missing assessed sets, and extra scored categories.

### SCAA Legacy response contract (`f=2`)

Sources: 21 November 2009 protocol pp. 3–7 for the scoring/procedure and the supplied undated official paper form for its field layout. The protocol's 16 listed quality marks are `6.00,6.25,…,9.75`; although the paper ruler prints `10` as an endpoint, do not add `10.00` as a selectable mark without a reviewed source-policy change. Name/date/sample number bind to envelope/session context, not new score fields. No quality-classification badge is defined because the historical references disagree.

| Canonical field | Type and allowed values | Completion rule / source meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `quality_ratings` | Fixed, ordered mapping `fragrance_aroma`, `flavor`, `aftertaste`, `acidity`, `body`, `balance`, `overall`; integer quarter-units `24…39` (display `6.00…9.75`) | All seven required; never substitute CVA `mouthfeel` for `body` or CVA `sweetness` for the cup-wise Legacy check. |
| `consistent_cups` | Explicit set of cup positions | Required for `n≥2`; score `10×|set|/n`. At `n=1`, form records `{1}` and derived Uniformity is always 10, without asking the taster to judge inter-cup consistency. |
| `sweet_cups`, `clean_cups` | Separate explicit sets of cup positions | Both assessed sets required, including explicit empty sets; each score `10×|set|/n`. A scored defect cup cannot be marked clean because Clean Cup excludes interfering negative impressions; no automatic Sweetness inference. |
| `scored_defect` | Explicit `null` or **one** object `{kind, description, affected_cups}`; absent in an unfinished draft only; `kind` exactly `taint` or `fault`; description non-blank text; affected set nonempty | At completion, explicit `null` means assessed no scored defect; **absence is unassessed, not no defect**. One form row avoids double-counting; `kind` maps to severity 2 or 4. Do not accept a list or multiple scored entries under this version. Other observations belong in unscored notes. |
| `roast_shade_tick` | Optional discrete integer `1…4`, top-to-bottom/lightest-to-darkest paper shade position | Unscored observation of the paper's four shaded roast-level marks, not a calibrated roast value or quality rating. Absence means no roast-shade observation. Do not collapse this to arbitrary free text in the canonical form. |
| `roast_level_note`, `aroma_qualities`, `notes` | Optional text | Additional paper-form observations, unscored; these do not replace the discrete roast mark. Blank permitted; no invented numeric roast calibration. |
| `dry_aroma_intensity`, `break_aroma_intensity`, `wet_aroma_intensity`, `acidity_intensity`, `body_level` | Optional ordinal `1…5` or absent | Unscored observational ticks. Dry and Break plus Acidity and Body appear on the paper; 2009 protocol p. 5 additionally describes wet aroma. Retain wet as an optional protocol-derived observation rather than silently omitting it or making a novel scored axis. Do not infer one intensity from a quality rating. |

The optional `wet_aroma_intensity` is the explicit resolution of the 2009-text-versus-undated-paper discrepancy, subject to independent source/UX review. `roast_shade_tick` is a source-faithful digital ordinal of the four visible paper shades, not a claim that SCAA defined numeric roast units; the exact visual labels/control need UX review. The intensity tick direction is likewise an observation, not an additional 0–10 quality score. UX may implement accessible discrete choices rather than a drag ruler, but must retain paper-form meanings and review any field omission.

Let `Q` be the sum of the seven quality ratings in quarter-units, `c=|consistent_cups|` (fixed `1` at `n=1`), `s=|sweet_cups|`, `k=|clean_cups|`, and `a=|scored_defect.affected_cups|` (zero when null). `U=10c/n`, `W=10s/n`, `C=10k/n`, `D=severity×a×5/n` (severity 2 taint or 4 fault), and exact `T=Q/4+U+W+C`, `F=T−D`. No second defect is summed. Use integer/fraction arithmetic throughout; round only final `F` to two decimal places using decimal half-up (exact halfway goes up). Component display may round to two decimals for readability but must not be re-summed to calculate `F`; retain exact values for storage/export and reproduce from observations. At `n≠5`, every total view/export must visibly say `Adapted — five-cup-equivalent Cup App score`. At `n=5`, five-cup component math matches the source's 2 points per qualifying cup and source defect multiplication.

Required score/validation fixtures: seven `7.00` ratings, all three cup sets full, `n=5`, no defect → `79.00`; same with one fault cup excluded from `clean_cups` → `73.00` (`Clean Cup=8`, defect deduction 4); seven `7.00`, `n=1`, all three sets `{1}`, one fault affecting that cup is **invalid** because clean/defect conflict; with `clean_cups={}` it is valid and final `49.00` (`49+10+10+0−20`); `n=3`, seven `7.00`, consistent `{1,2}`, sweet `{1,2}`, clean `{1,2}`, one taint affecting cup 3 gives exact `F=197/3≈65.666…`, display `65.67`. For additional no-defect recurring-fraction fixtures with seven `7.00` ratings: `n=6`, set sizes `(consistent,sweet,clean)=(5,4,5)` gives `217/3≈72.333…` → `72.33`; `n=7`, sizes `(6,5,7)` gives `523/7≈74.714…` → `74.71`; `n=8`, sizes `(7,6,8)` gives `75.25`.
