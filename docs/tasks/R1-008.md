## Decisions

- On 6 October 2026, PM and a separate read-only audit agent inspected GitHub-verified `origin/main` `0178d02168483e5391f11e98a5f9b656ead1fd24`. The prototype offers only `SCA CVA` per sample; `sessions` has no form/version; the same unversioned eight-score UI opens for every sample; unknown form values currently fall back to `1`. This is useful prototype scaffolding, not evidence of two compliant SCA forms. A new Legacy picker option alone would misrepresent results.
- On 6 October 2026 Andrew decided that the Release 1 `SCA CVA` option means **Affective Assessment only**. The current app's CVA screen is the prototype baseline to preserve where it works, not yet proof of exact conformity. Descriptive, Physical, and Extrinsic CVA assessments are not separate Release 1 forms. The repository contains an archived SCA-104 Affective Assessment September 2024 PDF, but the exact source-derived field/score contract and permission basis still require review. No form-specific implementation is assigned yet.
- SCA's [CVA Brand Usage Guidelines](https://sca.coffee/cva-brand-usage-guidelines) state that unauthorised whole or partial use of CVA materials/forms in products or services is forbidden unless explicitly authorised by SCA. The archived SCA-104 PDF also conditions reproduction on consent and attribution. On 6 October 2026 Andrew explicitly confirmed that he has this permission for Cup App and, when asked separately, confirmed that his SCA permission also covers use of the historical SCAA Legacy form in Cup App. These are Product Owner-reported permissions; the underlying grant, scope, conditions, and attribution requirements have not been inspected or copied into this task. The team must respect any applicable terms before distribution; this is not a legal conclusion.
- Andrew supplied local copies of SCAA-branded original materials in the Google Drive folder `My Drive/Work/Digital Cupping/SCA Assement Methods/2004(old)/`. The exact portable filenames and SHA-256 hashes below identify the reviewed copies; they are not added to this repository. The form itself is undated and its publication provenance is not established. The 2005 and 2009 protocols provide edition evidence, but a single canonical Legacy edition remains to be selected. The original form has seven quarter-point quality attributes, five cup-wise checks each for Sweetness, Uniformity, and Clean Cup, and taint/fault deductions. Its marked/ticked input is materially different from the existing CVA Affective simple score buttons. A mobile interaction can adapt the paper ruler to accessible tap targets, but must preserve the original field and scoring meaning; the detailed interaction needs UX review against FR-032 and `docs/UI_STYLE_GUIDE.md`. The 2005/2009 protocols prescribe at least five prepared cups for uniformity and use five cup-wise checks for the standard total; Cup App's 1–8 workflow needs explicit rules for the remaining cup-wise components and result labelling outside the five-cup case.
- Andrew rejected a proposed five-cup-only Legacy restriction and explicitly requires **1–8 physical cups per sample for both Release 1 forms**, matching the existing `CUP_NUMBER_OPTIONS` selector in `src/features/cupping/constants/sessionDetails.js`. This decides setup/capture range, not the complete Legacy total-score calculation or claim of historical SCAA comparability. The 2005/2009 protocols award 2 points per cup for Sweetness, Uniformity, and Clean Cup, to a maximum of 10; therefore non-five-cup results need a clear calculation and label. Do not silently extend the Uniformity rule to other fields or present an adapted total as an unqualified standard SCAA score.
- Andrew decided that **Legacy Uniformity is always 10 with one cup**. For `n = 2…8`, its score is `10 × c ÷ n`, with `c` the integer count of cups the taster judges consistent, `0 ≤ c ≤ n`. Thus five cups reproduce the source's 2-points-per-consistent-cup rule; for example, three of four judged consistent score 7.5. This is a Cup App adaptation for other cup counts, not a claim that the historical form specified them. No rounding or storage representation is approved yet; retain enough source observations to reproduce the score.
