The archived SCA-104 PDF also conditions reproduction on consent and attribution. On 6 October 2026 Andrew explicitly confirmed that he has this permission for Cup App and, when asked separately, confirmed that his SCA permission also covers use of the historical SCAA Legacy form in Cup App. These are Product Owner-reported permissions; the underlying grant, scope, conditions, and attribution requirements have not been inspected or copied into this task. The team must respect any applicable terms before distribution; this is not a legal conclusion.
- Andrew supplied local copies of SCAA-branded original materials in the Google Drive folder `My Drive/Work/Digital Cupping/SCA Assement Methods/2004(old)/`. The exact portable filenames and SHA-256 hashes below identify the reviewed copies; they are not added to this repository. The form itself is undated and its publication provenance is not established. The 2005 and 2009 protocols provide edition evidence, but a single canonical Legacy edition remains to be selected. The original form has seven quarter-point quality attributes, five cup-wise checks each for Sweetness, Uniformity, and Clean Cup, and taint/fault deductions. Its marked/ticked input is materially different from the existing CVA Affective simple score buttons. A mobile interaction can adapt the paper ruler to accessible tap targets, but must preserve the original field and scoring meaning; the detailed interaction needs UX review against FR-032 and `docs/UI_STYLE_GUIDE.md`. The 2005/2009 protocols prescribe at least five prepared cups for uniformity and use five cup-wise checks for the standard total. They do not prescribe a non-five-cup calculation; the Cup App adaptations below are explicit Product Owner choices.
- Andrew rejected a proposed five-cup-only Legacy restriction and explicitly requires **1–8 physical cups per sample for both Release 1 forms**, matching the existing `CUP_NUMBER_OPTIONS` selector in `src/features/cupping/constants/sessionDetails.js`. This decides setup/capture range, not the complete Legacy form-definition contract or claim of historical SCAA comparability. The 2005/2009 protocols award 2 points per cup for Sweetness, Uniformity, and Clean Cup, to a maximum of 10. Do not present a non-five-cup total as an unqualified standard SCAA score.
- Andrew decided that **Legacy Uniformity is always 10 with one cup**. For `n = 2…8`, its score is `10 × c ÷ n`, with `c` the integer count of cups the taster judges consistent, `0 ≤ c ≤ n`. Thus five cups reproduce the source's 2-points-per-consistent-cup rule; for example, three of four judged consistent score 7.5. This is a Cup App adaptation for other cup counts, not a claim that the historical form specified them. No rounding or storage representation is approved yet; retain enough source observations to reproduce the score.
- After considering the historical protocol and online examples, Andrew approved the same proportional cup-count approach **for now** for Legacy Sweetness and Clean Cup: each attribute scores `10 × q ÷ n`, where `q` is the number of cups judged to qualify and `n = 1…8`. For one cup each is 0 or 10 depending on that cup; the automatic 10 exception belongs only to Uniformity. With five cups, all three rules reduce to 2 points per qualifying cup. A non-five-cup Legacy total, if displayed, must be clearly labelled as a Cup App adaptation. This is the current Product Owner decision pending final form-contract review; it does not define rounding or the complete final-total contract.
- Andrew then decided to scale Legacy **taint and fault deductions** to a five-cup equivalent for every supported cup count: for each defect, `severity × affected_cups × 5 ÷ total_cups`, with severity 2 for a taint or 4 for a fault. Thus one fault affecting the only cup deducts 20, whereas one fault among five deducts 4. Five-cup calculations match the historical form; other counts are Cup App adaptations. Preserve actual affected-cup observations and the selected `n` so the deduction is reproducible. This does not choose rounding, allow silently double-counting the same event, or complete every validation rule.
- After a read-only design audit and independent source review, the PM selected SCAA Protocols **VERSION 21NOV2009A** as the Legacy scoring/procedure reference, the undated `SCAA-Official-Cupping-Form.pdf` as the field/layout reference, and the April 2005 protocol as corroboration. The seven Legacy quality ratings are the protocol's explicit 16 quarter-point values `6.00…9.75`; the paper ruler's printed `10` endpoint is recorded as a source ambiguity, not silently made selectable. The 2005/2009 quality-classification tables differ, so no category badge is in this contract. The paper form supplies one scored defect assessment per sample (description, taint/fault severity, affected cup set); other observations may be notes. Multiple independently scored defect entries require a separate Product Owner policy, not an implementation-agent invention.
- The pure **Legacy** scoring contract should retain the seven quality ratings as integer quarter-units and all per-cup judgments/affected-cup IDs. Calculate with exact fractions and round only the displayed Legacy final number to two decimal places using a documented deterministic rounding rule; do not sum rounded component displays. Test recurring-fraction counts including `n=3,6,7,8`. This is a reversible PM/architecture design choice, not an SCA-prescribed numeric representation and not a change to CVA's source-prescribed quarter-point final rounding.
- The 2009 text mentions wet-aroma intensity while the supplied undated paper form visibly offers Dry and Break intensity axes. The full Legacy field contract must state the resolution explicitly; an implementation agent must not silently omit a required source field or add an unreviewed control.
- The CVA Affective source is archived SCA Standard **104-2024** (`archive/original-root-app/docs/AW_SCA-104_Affective-Assessment_Sept2024_Secured.pdf`, SHA-256 `c788f986e938d3b10786ce0ee62462b21aec0ed2cfdce6de9f090e0d1b491c75`). Its 8 one-to-nine ratings and five-cup defect/non-uniform deductions need a separate source-grounded immutable contract. The prototype currently multiplies non-uniform and defective cup counts by `5 ÷ n` before applying the source's `-2`/`-4` coefficients; this changes a one-cup defective deduction from 4 to 20. Andrew approved 1–8 physical cups but has **not yet explicitly decided this CVA scoring adaptation**. The Legacy decision does not transfer automatically. Hold the affected CVA form-definition freeze until Andrew decides; preserve the current UI as a baseline, not conformance proof.
