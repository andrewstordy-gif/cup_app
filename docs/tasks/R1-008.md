## Decisions

- On 6 October 2026, PM and a separate read-only audit agent inspected GitHub-verified `origin/main` `0178d02168483e5391f11e98a5f9b656ead1fd24`. The prototype offers only `SCA CVA` per sample; `sessions` has no form/version; the same unversioned eight-score UI opens for every sample; unknown form values currently fall back to `1`. This is useful prototype scaffolding, not evidence of two compliant SCA forms. A new Legacy picker option alone would misrepresent results.
- SCA's current [CVA overview](https://sca.coffee/value-assessment) describes separate Affective and Descriptive assessments. The repository contains an archived SCA-104 Affective Assessment September 2024 PDF, but no complete approved two-form field/score contract. Andrew's CVA coverage decision has been requested and remains pending. No implementation is assigned until the decision and source basis are recorded.
- SCA's [CVA Brand Usage Guidelines](https://sca.coffee/cva-brand-usage-guidelines) state that unauthorised whole or partial use of CVA materials/forms in products or services is forbidden unless explicitly authorised by SCA. The archived SCA-104 PDF also conditions reproduction on consent and attribution. The PM does not infer a Cup App licence from public downloadability; Andrew must confirm an authorisation route before the team implements or distributes SCA-branded CVA form content. This is a permissions escalation, not a legal conclusion about independent functional concepts.
- A separate read-only Legacy source audit identified the original SCA-authored [Arabica Cupping Form](https://www.scith.coffee/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/SCA-Cupping-Form.pdf) and earlier SCAA-authored [cupping protocol](https://coffeenate.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/09/PR-CUPPING-PROTOCOLS-V.21NOV2009A.pdf), both hosted by third parties. They are research leads, not yet an edition-proven or permission-cleared contract. Their scoring shape differs materially from the prototype: seven quarter-point quality attributes, three cup-wise attributes, and explicit taint/fault deductions. The source protocol assumes at least five cups for uniformity, whereas Cup App supports smaller physical-cup workflows; policy for an official Legacy score with fewer than five cups remains a Product Owner decision before that flow is activated.
- Later vertical tasks should implement session-level selection and form-specific capture/review while preserving the established UI. They must address existing per-sample selection and unsupported-value fallback without silently changing prototype records. This task does not pre-approve a schema or migration plan.
